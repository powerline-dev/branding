## Usage Guidelines

- Preserve the original color order.
- Use indigo as the starting color.
- Use golden yellow as the ending color.
- Apply the palette from left to right.
- For wordmarks, assign individual colors to characters.
- Interpolate colors when adapting the palette to different text lengths.
- Avoid introducing unrelated colors into official brand assets.

Product-specific statusline themes and user-customizable colors are not restricted by this palette.
